Where the reading journey began — session one of the Hitchhiker’s Guide to Agentic AI, online and free.

## What we covered

Some topics are best learned together, out loud. We opened our book-reading series on agentic AI with the foundations — read, debated, and questioned as a group.

- **What an agent actually is** — goals, tools, environment, and the loop that connects them.
- **Why now** — what changed to make agents work: models, context windows, and tool protocols.
- **The reading format** — how the series works: short sections, live discussion, zero lectures.

## What we built

A shared vocabulary and a reading circle — 25 learners finishing session one with the same mental model of agentic systems and a plan for the weeks ahead.

## Takeaways

- Reading together beats reading alone: every confusing paragraph meets someone who just figured it out.
- Agents demystify fast once you name the loop: perceive, decide, act, observe.
- The series is open to everyone, online, and free — show up having read nothing and leave having learned plenty.
