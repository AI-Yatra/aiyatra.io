Book-reading series finale: agentic patterns, discussion and reading together — online, open to everyone.

## What we covered

Four sessions, one arc. The finale tied the whole series together — from "what is an agent" to patterns the community now uses in real builds.

- **The full pattern map** — tools, planning, memory, workflows: how the pieces compose.
- **From reading to building** — which series ideas already showed up in Saturday labs (Goose, harness engineering, guardrails).
- **Where next** — the community voted with its curiosity: what the next reading series should take on.

## What we built

A finished series and a room of 40 readers-turned-builders — the finale doubled as a launchpad into the hands-on agentic AI sessions that followed.

## Takeaways

- A reading series works when it ends in a build queue, not just a bookshelf.
- 40 people on a Monday evening proves the format: short, live, discussed — never lectured.
- The Hitchhiker's Guide arc now lives on as the conceptual backbone of our agent sessions.
