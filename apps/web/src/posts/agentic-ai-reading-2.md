Session two of the reading series — continuing the guided tour through agentic AI, online in the evening.

## What we covered

With the foundations set, session two went deeper into how agents are actually put together — the patterns that separate a demo from a system.

- **Tools and function calling** — how agents reach outside the model: schemas, validation, and failure modes.
- **Planning vs. reacting** — when to decompose up front and when to let the loop figure it out.
- **Memory that matters** — what to keep, what to summarize, and what to throw away between steps.

## What we built

Shared notes and a growing pattern catalogue — 35 learners comparing implementations and war stories from their own experiments between sessions.

## Takeaways

- Most agent failures are tool-design failures, not model failures.
- Planning is a spectrum: the right point on it depends on the task, not the fashion.
- Evening online sessions hit different — 35 people showed up mid-week for this one.
