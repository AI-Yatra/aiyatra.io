Session three of the Hitchhiker’s Guide series — agents, tools and workflows, read and debated together.

## What we covered

Small room, deep discussion. Session three took on the workflow question: how do agent steps compose into reliable work?

- **Single-agent vs. multi-agent** — when one loop suffices and when to split roles.
- **Handoffs and supervision** — patterns for agents checking each other's work.
- **Workflows as code** — where the graph is explicit, where it is emergent, and how to test either.

## What we built

A whiteboard of workflow patterns argued over by 16 regulars — the kind of session where everyone talks and the notes write themselves.

## Takeaways

- Small sessions go deepest: 16 people, zero lurkers, every voice in the mix.
- Multi-agent is an organizational pattern first and a technical one second.
- If you cannot draw the workflow on a whiteboard, you cannot debug it in production.
